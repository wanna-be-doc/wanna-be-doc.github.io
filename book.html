The Book

So You Want to Be a Doctor

All About the Human Body — a middle school introduction to thinking like a physician.

Synopsis

How doctors really think

Written for middle school readers, So You Want to Be a Doctor introduces the foundations of anatomy and physiology alongside the reasoning process doctors use every day: asking thoughtful questions, recognizing symptoms, and thinking critically about evidence. Richly illustrated throughout, the book pairs approachable explanations with case by case diagnostic questions at the end of each chapter to reinforce learning.
