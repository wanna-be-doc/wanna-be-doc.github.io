School Partnerships

12 communities and counting

Through partnerships with local Rotary clubs and schools, the book has been placed in classrooms and libraries across eight countries — supported by volunteers on the ground, with new partnerships forming as the initiative continues to expand its global reach.

Hospital Partners

Reaching young patients too

The book has also found a home in children's hospitals, where it introduces young patients to the world of medicine from the other side of the stethoscope.

Distribution Partners

A partnership built for scale

Distribution is made possible through Rotary International's global network, Books for Africa, Book Aid International Swindon (UK), and RA Foundation (Mumbai) — working with local clubs, schools, and communities to identify those in need of health literacy resources and to make sure books reach the students who will benefit most.

Growth Timeline

How the initiative has expanded

Phase 1

First copies distributed in the United States

Initial 40 copies shared within local communities and schools.

Phase 2

Distribution partnerships begin

Collaborations formalized with Rotary International, Books for Africa, Book Aid International Swindon (UK), and RA Foundation (Mumbai) to distribute books globally.

Phase 3

Expansion to India

410 copies distributed across schools and communities in India.

Phase 4

Reach extends to Africa

40 copies each distributed in Ghana, Nigeria, and Rwanda.

Phase 5

Rotary Friendship Exchange reaches Mexico

6 copies shared with Rotary Friendship Exchange delegates from Mexico.

616 books · 8 countries · growing
