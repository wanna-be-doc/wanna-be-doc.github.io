Mission

Making the language of medicine feel within reach.

Sanjana believes every student deserves a clear, honest introduction to how doctors think — not just what they know. Her work blends storytelling, illustration, and inquiry-based learning to open the door to medicine, neuroscience, and health literacy for the next generation of curious minds.

🧠

Scientific Curiosity

Encouraging students to ask thoughtful questions about how the human body works.

Health Literacy
